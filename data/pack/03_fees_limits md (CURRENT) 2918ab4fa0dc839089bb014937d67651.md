All fees are charged to the **sender** unless stated otherwise. Amounts in KBR.

## Person-to-person (RiverPay to RiverPay)

**0.5%** of the send amount, **minimum 1 KBR**, **maximum 25 KBR**.

- Send 100 KBR → fee 1 KBR (minimum).
- Send 1,000 KBR → fee 5 KBR.
- Send 10,000 KBR → fee 25 KBR (cap), not 50.

Receiving P2P is **free**.

## Agent cash-in (customer)

**Free** for the customer.

## Agent cash-out (customer)

**1%** of the cash-out amount, **minimum 5 KBR**. No maximum cap.

Example: cash out 200 KBR → fee 5 KBR (minimum). Cash out 2,000 KBR → fee 20 KBR.

## Failed transfers

If ledger status is FAILED and no debit posted, **no fee**. If a fee was taken on a transfer that later failed, it is reversed within **1 business day**.

## What is not in this product

- No promotional “zero fee Fridays.”
- No USD/EUR FX table. Do not quote an exchange rate.
