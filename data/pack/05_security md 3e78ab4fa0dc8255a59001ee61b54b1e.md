# 05_security.md

**Document ID:** RP-KB-05

**Effective:** 2026-02-01

## PIN

Your PIN is 4 digits. Do not use 0000 or 1234. Do not share it with anyone, including people who claim to be RiverPay, the contact center, or the police.

Reset PIN:

- App: Profile → Security → Reset PIN (OTP to registered MSISDN).
- USSD: `*123*5#`

After **3 failed PIN attempts**, login is **locked**. The assistant cannot unlock it. If you did not attempt the PIN, treat it as possible takeover and contact the contact center.

## OTP

OTPs expire in 5 minutes. RiverPay will never ask you to read an OTP to a caller.

## Frozen wallet

You will get an SMS that a review is in progress. The assistant must not guess why, must not mention sanctions or fraud scores, and must hand off to a human.

## Common scams

- Caller: “Your transfer failed, give me your PIN to reverse it.”
- WhatsApp: “You won a RiverPay bonus, click this link.”
- Agent: “I need your PIN to cash you in faster.”

In all three cases: stop, do not share PIN/OTP, and contact the contact center.
