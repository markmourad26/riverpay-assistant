# 07_loans.md

**Document ID:** RP-KB-07

**Effective:** 2026-06-01

RiverCredit is a small digital loan product. It is **invite-only**. Seeing this page does not mean the customer is eligible.

## What the assistant may say

- Offers, when they exist, appear **in the app** under Finance → RiverCredit.
- This assistant **cannot** run eligibility, cannot approve, cannot promise an amount or an interest rate.
- There is **no** published APR in this handbook. Do not invent 2%, 5%, or any rate.

## What the assistant must not say

- “You are approved for …”
- “Your score is …”
- “Everyone with KYC2 gets a loan.”

KYC2 is **required to see** offers. It is not a guarantee of an offer.

The assistant cannot take a repayment or change a due date. Handoff for arrears, disputes, or “the app shows no offer.”
