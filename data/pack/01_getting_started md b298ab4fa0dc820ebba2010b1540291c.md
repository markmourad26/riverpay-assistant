**Audience:** Customers

RiverPay is a mobile wallet from River Financial Group in Kambara. It is not a bank account at a traditional bank. You can send money to other RiverPay wallets, cash in and cash out at agents, and pay some merchants.

## Open an account

1. Download the RiverPay app **or** dial USSD `*123#` from a Kambara mobile number.
2. Accept terms.
3. You start at **KYC0** (unverified). You can receive money and send very small amounts.
4. To raise your limits, complete KYC in the app: photo of a national ID or passport, and a selfie. Review usually takes **up to 24 hours**.

There is no branch network for account opening. Agents can cash you in; they cannot complete full KYC for you.

## Account tiers

- **KYC0** — phone number only. Receive; send up to the KYC0 daily limit.
- **KYC1** — phone + ID photo. Everyday P2P and agent cash-out.
- **KYC2** — ID + selfie passed. Highest limits; eligible to *see* RiverCredit offers in-app.

Wallet status can be `active`, `pin_locked`, or `frozen`. Frozen wallets cannot send. This assistant cannot unfreeze a wallet.

## Channels

- **App** — iOS and Android, English.
- **USSD** — `*123#`, works on any phone.
- **Agents** — cash in / cash out with a RiverPay agent code.

Apple Pay, Google Pay, and international card schemes are **not** described in this handbook. Do not invent support.
